# Camera and 3D

Use `CueMovingCameraScene` for `self.camera.frame.animate` in 2D. Use `CueThreeDScene` with `ThreeDAxes`, `set_camera_orientation`, and `move_camera` for spatial explanations. Use Manim radians and `DEGREES`. Use `add_fixed_in_frame_mobjects` for screen-aligned labels. Choose a readable projection, use modest surface resolution, and keep the camera stable while explaining a relationship.
